Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

Who created FinTrack?
FinTrack is an engineering project conceived, designed, and developed by Md Rounaq Ali. It was built to demonstrate enterprise-grade full-stack architecture, relational database management, and responsive UI engineering.
Is FinTrack completely free?
Yes! FinTrack is 100% free to use. There are no paid tiers, no advertisements, and no paywalls.
How does FinTrack secure my financial data?
All passwords are encrypted with industry-standard Werkzeug SHA-256 cryptographic salts. Email verification OTPs expire in 10 minutes and lock after 5 incorrect attempts. The production database is isolated on Clever Cloud with encrypted SSL connections.
Can I use multiple currencies?
Yes! FinTrack supports customizable currency symbols (₹, $, €, £, ¥, etc.), which you can update at any time under your Profile Settings.
